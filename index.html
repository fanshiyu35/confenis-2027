Welcome

The CONFENIS series, revived in Vienna

CONFENIS is the flagship conference of IFIP Working Group 8.9 on Enterprise Information Systems. After hiatus following the 2019 edition, the series resumes at TU Wien in 2027 — anchored in the European tradition of rigorous, formal, peer-reviewed information systems research.

Rigorous peer review

Double-blind, three reviewers per paper, drawn from a 90-member international Programme Committee. Acceptance rate target: 28%. Methodological and theoretical contributions weighted equally with empirical work.

Springer LNBIP proceedings

Accepted full papers published in Lecture Notes in Business Information Processing, indexed in Scopus, DBLP, EI, Google Scholar, Web of Science Conference Proceedings Citation Index.

European academic setting

Hosted at TU Wien — one of Europe's oldest technical universities, founded 1815, in the heart of imperial Vienna. Sessions held in the Kuppelsaal and Boecklsaal of the historic Karlsplatz main building.

Conference themes

CONFENIS 2027 invites research contributions across the full spectrum of Enterprise Information Systems, with particular attention to the strategic priorities of the European digital agenda.

  • ERP modernisation and post-modern ERP architectures
  • Process mining, conformance checking and predictive analytics
  • Enterprise architecture and digital twin platforms
  • EIS for sustainability and ESG compliance (CSRD, EU Taxonomy)
  • AI-augmented business process management
  • Inter-organisational systems, data spaces, and GAIA-X
  • Cybersecurity and resilience of mission-critical EIS
  • Human-centric enterprise systems and digital sovereignty
