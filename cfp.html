Call for Papers

Submit to CONFENIS 2027 — Springer LNBIP proceedings

Full papers, short papers, posters, and doctoral consortium submissions are invited across all areas of Enterprise Information Systems research and practice.

Scope and themes

CONFENIS 2027 invites original, unpublished research contributions across the full breadth of Enterprise Information Systems. Submissions are clustered into nine thematic tracks. Each track is curated by two area chairs and reviewed by a dedicated subset of the Programme Committee.

Track 1 — ERP and post-modern enterprise architectures

S/4HANA migration, composable ERP, microservices in the enterprise core, ERP and AI-as-a-service integration, legacy modernisation strategies.

Track 2 — Business process management and process mining

Process discovery, conformance and enhancement, predictive process monitoring, object-centric event logs, concept drift, RPA and intelligent automation.

Track 3 — Enterprise architecture and digital twins

ArchiMate and TOGAF in practice, enterprise digital twins, simulation-based architecture analysis, organisation-platform co-design.

Track 4 — Sustainability and ESG-aware EIS

Embedding CSRD, EU Taxonomy and SFDR reporting in ERP and EPM systems, carbon accounting in supply-chain IS, sustainability data quality.

Track 5 — AI-augmented enterprise systems

LLMs in EIS workflows, retrieval-augmented enterprise search, AI agents over SAP/Oracle/Dynamics, evaluation frameworks for enterprise AI.

Track 6 — Inter-organisational systems and data spaces

GAIA-X, IDS, Catena-X, federated identities, sovereign data exchange, cross-border B2B integration, data product engineering.

Track 7 — Cybersecurity and resilience of EIS

NIS2 implementation in enterprise stacks, EIS incident response, supply-chain cyber risk, identity and access management at scale.

Track 8 — Human-centric EIS and digital workplace

User experience of enterprise systems, change management, neurodiversity-aware EIS design, hybrid work platforms, digital sovereignty for end users.

Track 9 — Public-sector and government EIS

Once-Only Principle, eIDAS 2.0, government ERP, GovTech procurement, vendor lock-in mitigation.

Submission system

All submissions are handled through EasyChair at easychair.org/conferences/?conf=confenis2027. Authors must use the Springer LNBIP LaTeX template (preferred) or the LNBIP Word template. PDFs only; no other formats accepted.

Review process

CONFENIS 2027 uses a fully double-blind review process. Authors must not identify themselves in the manuscript, in the metadata, or in self-references (use "Author (2024) showed..." rather than "We (Author 2024) showed..."). Each paper receives three independent reviews, followed by a meta-review by the relevant area chair. Programme co-chairs make the final accept/reject decision.

Author obligations

  • Each accepted paper requires at least one author registration at the full rate.
  • At least one author must present the paper in person in Vienna; hybrid presentation is not offered.
  • Authors transfer copyright to Springer under the standard LNBIP consent-to-publish; open-access option available.
